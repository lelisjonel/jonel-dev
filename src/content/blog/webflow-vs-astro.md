I build websites in both **Webflow** and **Astro**, and clients often ask which
one they should choose. The honest answer: it depends on who will edit the site
and what matters most to the business.

## The short version

| | Webflow | Astro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Editing** | Visual editor, great for marketing teams | Code and Markdown files |
| **Speed** | Fast | Extremely fast, plain static HTML |
| **Hosting cost** | Monthly site plan | Often free (Vercel, Netlify) |
| **CMS** | Built in | Markdown, or connect a headless CMS |
| **Best for** | Teams that update content often | Developers, blogs, docs, max performance |

## When I recommend Webflow

Choose Webflow when your **marketing team needs to update pages themselves**:
new landing pages, blog posts, case studies. Its visual editor and CMS make that
easy, and with Client-First structure the site stays organized as it grows.

## When I recommend Astro

Choose Astro when **speed, ownership and low running costs** matter most, and
the people editing content are comfortable with simple files. Astro sends plain
HTML to the browser, so pages load almost instantly. This portfolio is built
with Astro for exactly that reason.

## What about SEO?

Both can rank well. What matters more is clean semantic HTML, fast load times,
good titles and descriptions, and useful content, and I build all of those into
every site, whichever platform you choose.
