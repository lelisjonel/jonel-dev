## The goal

Learn Astro by building something real: a marketing site with a blog,
categories, authors and pagination, all without a CMS subscription.

## What I built

- File-based routing for Home, About, Contact and the Blog
- Content collections as a "code CMS" with a typed schema
- Paginated blog with category and author pages
- Automatic deploys to Netlify on every push

## Result

A site that loads instantly, costs nothing to host, and keeps all
content in plain Markdown files I own.
