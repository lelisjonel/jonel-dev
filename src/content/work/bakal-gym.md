## The goal

A concept site for a fictional gym that needed to feel loud and confident,
and push visitors to one action: claim a free trial class.

## What I built

- A bold hero with an animated barbell plate and a clear free trial call to action
- Programs, a weekly class schedule, coaches and membership pricing with no contracts
- Member results and an FAQ section
- A fully responsive single-page layout

## Result

A fast, single-page site where every section leads back to the free trial.
