## The project

DesignXP is a Figma-approved partner program that places aspiring UX designers
on real product work with startups and AI companies. The site needed to explain
the apprenticeship clearly and turn visitors into applicants.

## What I built

- A conversion-focused hero with a pricing card and clear "Apply now" calls to action
- An auto-scrolling "Backed & Supported" logo marquee
- CMS-driven listings for current apprenticeships, shipped apprentice work and blog posts
- A step-by-step "How it works" section, reviews and FAQs
- Fully responsive layouts across desktop, tablet and mobile
