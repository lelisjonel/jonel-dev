## The project

A single-page campaign by the security company Jazz, giving security
professionals a place to share frustrations with data loss prevention (DLP) tools.

## What I built

- A "wall" of anonymous stories and quotes from security teams
- A story submission form integrated with HubSpot
- Calls to action for the protest kit store, the report and live events
- A punchy, campaign-style layout that works on every screen size
