## The goal

Explore how far a static Astro site can go with motion. Field Theory is a
fictional multi-sport club and league, and the site needed to feel like the
sports it sells.

## What I built

- A real-time 3D ball made with Three.js that you can drag, and click to switch between basketball, tennis, soccer and volleyball
- Scroll-driven animations with GSAP and smooth scrolling with Lenis
- Sports, moments and program sections with level-based plans
- A light and dark mode toggle
- Reduced-motion support for visitors who turn animations off

## Result

A playful, interactive site that still loads as static HTML.
