## The project

Giving Green researches and recommends the most effective climate nonprofits.
The site brings together giving options, research reports, news and events, so
it changes often and needs to stay accurate and polished.

## What I do

- Publish and update CMS content: research reports, news posts and nonprofit recommendations
- Set up campaign pieces like event and webinar popups for giving seasons
- Update pages and sections inside the existing structure so new content matches the rest of the site
- QA every change on desktop, tablet and mobile before it goes live

## Why maintenance matters

For a nonprofit, the website is where donations happen. Keeping it fast,
accurate and up to date, especially around big giving moments, directly
supports their mission.
