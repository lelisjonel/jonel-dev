## The project

Giving Green researches and recommends the most effective climate nonprofits.
The site brings together giving options, research reports and news, so donors
can give with confidence.

## What I built

- "Ways to Make an Impact" cards for the Giving Green Fund, top nonprofits and supporting the research
- Impact stats and a "Featured In" press section
- A CMS-driven research library and news listing
- Site search, an event popup and dropdown navigation across a large multi-page site
