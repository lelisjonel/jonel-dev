## The project

A website for a VA staffing company with talent in the Philippines and the
Dominican Republic, built to win clients and attract remote applicants.

## What I built

- Service pages for eight VA categories, from customer service to medical billing
- A clear six-step hiring process section
- A Careers page listing open remote roles
- About, Blog and Contact pages with clear calls to action
