## The project

A redesign of the corporate website for KITCI, a Pasig City supplier of passive
fire protection, insulation, raised flooring and refractory products.

## What I built

- Multi-page structure: About, Products, Services, Projects, Blog and Contact
- A project gallery and company stats that build trust with industrial clients
- A "KITCI Toolbox" calculations page and a downloadable company profile
- Contact and newsletter forms, plus a CMS-driven blog
