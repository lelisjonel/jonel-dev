## The goal

Show how a full sales funnel works, step by step. Launch Lab is a fictional
5-day email course on building landing pages that convert.

## What I built

- An opt-in page with a lead magnet and sign-up form
- An offer page, a checkout, a one-click upsell and a welcome page
- A progress bar that shows each visitor where they are in the funnel
- Funnel state kept in the browser, so choices carry from step to step
- A demo setup: no emails are sent and no payments are taken

## Result

A clear, working example of a funnel I can adapt for real launches in Webflow or Astro.
