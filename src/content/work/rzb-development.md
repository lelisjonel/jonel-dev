## The project

RZB Development Corp is a PCAB- and DTI-certified general contractor and glass
and aluminum specialist in Baliuag, Bulacan. Their old Webflow site didn't show
the scale of their work: curtain walls on 55-storey towers, their own
fabrication plant and nearly two decades of projects across the Philippines.

## What I built

- A bold, industrial redesign that keeps only the RZB logo and brand colors
- The Webflow CMS moved into Astro content collections: 44 projects, services, products, glass types and blog posts
- Filterable project and product listings, detailed project spec sheets and galleries
- Quote, residential and job application forms with Web3Forms
- A residential landing page for homeowners, with a process and FAQ section
- SEO and AEO setup: per-page meta and Open Graph images, structured data, sitemap and llms.txt
- Mobile-first layouts, checked page by page at 320–390px

## Result

75 static pages that load fast, cost nothing to host on Vercel, and are easy
for the team to update from Markdown files.
