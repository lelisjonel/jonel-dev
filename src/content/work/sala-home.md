## The goal

Build a complete shopping flow without a commerce platform. Sala is a fictional
furniture brand selling solid wood pieces for every room.

## What I built

- Home, shop and product detail pages, with shopping by room
- Product variants like colors and finishes
- A cart that remembers items with localStorage, plus a cart drawer
- A demo checkout flow (no real payments)
- Editorial sections that tell the story behind the materials

## Result

A shop that feels like a real store, built as a static site that is free to host.
