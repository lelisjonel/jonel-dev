## The goal

A cozy site for a fictional neighborhood café that makes people want to visit.

## What I built

- A homepage with the café's story and beans from three Philippine regions
- A menu page with the drinks and food everyone orders
- New roast announcements, customer quotes and a visit section with hours
- Responsive layouts with a warm, editorial feel

## Result

A small, fast site that does what a café site needs: show the menu and get people through the door.
