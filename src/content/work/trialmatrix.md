## The project

TrialMatrix is an AI platform that maps every fact, witness and contradiction in
a legal case. The site had to feel premium and trustworthy for law firms, while
explaining a complex product in simple steps.

## What I built

- A full-bleed cinematic hero with a law-firm logo marquee
- Product feature sections (Case Graph, Case Strategy, Contradiction Detection) with step-by-step accordions
- A security section built for enterprise buyers
- FAQ and "Request a pilot" calls to action, plus About and Security pages
