## The project

A pre-launch landing page for yeebee, an app where prices drop as more people
pledge on a product. The goal: grow the waitlist before launch.

## What I built

- A four-step "how it works" section that explains the concept quickly
- Waitlist calls to action and a Discord community invite
- A FAQ section and "coming soon" App Store and Google Play badges
- A playful, energetic layout that matches the brand
